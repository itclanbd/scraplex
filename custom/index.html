Questions

Questions about custom projects

How fast is the pilot?

Most pilots are ready in two to three working days.

Who owns the data and the code?

You own the data we deliver. Scraper code can be handed over on request as part of the quote.

Can you scrape sites behind a login?

Sometimes, when you have the right to access the data. We review each case during the brief.

What happens when a site changes?

Our monitoring flags failed runs and we fix the scraper. Maintenance is part of every monthly plan.
