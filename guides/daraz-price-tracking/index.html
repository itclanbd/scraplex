Daraz Price Tracking: Monitor Competitor Prices in Bangladesh, Pakistan and More

Set up automated Daraz price tracking across BD, PK, LK, NP and MM. Track competitor prices, discounts and stock daily in Google Sheets, with no code.

By the Scraplex team at ITclan BDUpdated 14 Nov 20266 min read
Short answerRun the Daraz Product Scraper on a daily schedule for your category or competitor search, and send each run to Google Sheets. Every row has a scrapedAt timestamp, so you build a price and discount history for each product automatically. Tracking 1,000 products a day costs about $1 a day.

Prices on Daraz change often, especially around campaigns like 11.11 and 12.12. Sellers who react late lose sales, and the search ranking that comes with them. A simple daily price tracker shows you what competitors charge, when they discount and when they run out of stock.

Why track prices on Daraz

  • See competitor price changes the same day, not when your sales drop.
  • Spot discount patterns before big campaigns.
  • Know when a rival goes out of stock, so you can raise visibility while they are gone.
  • Check that resellers keep to your recommended price.

Set up daily tracking in 4 steps

  1. Choose what to track. A category URL covers a whole market segment. A keyword search covers products that compete with yours.
  2. Configure the Daraz Product Scraper. Set the country domain, add your URLs or keywords, and set maxProducts to the number of items you want to watch.
  3. Schedule it. In Apify, create a schedule that runs the scraper every morning.
  4. Send results to Google Sheets. Add the Google Sheets integration so each run appends new rows to the same sheet.

How to read the price history

Each row carries productId, price, originalPrice, discountPct, inStock and scrapedAt. In Google Sheets, a pivot table with products as rows and dates as columns turns this into a price grid. Add conditional formatting to highlight any drop of 10% or more since yesterday.

QuestionFields to use
Who cut their price today?price, scrapedAt
How deep are campaign discounts?originalPrice, discountPct
Who ran out of stock?inStock, sellerName
Which products sell fastest?soldCount over time

Tracking flash sales and campaigns

During campaigns, run the schedule more often, for example every four hours, for the products that matter most. Keep includeOutOfStock on, because sell-outs during a sale are useful demand data.

When to move to a custom feed

A scheduled scraper is enough for most sellers. Teams that track several markets, need product matching across sellers, or want alerts in Slack or email usually move to a custom data feed, where we deliver cleaned, matched data to a database or dashboard and maintain it for them.
