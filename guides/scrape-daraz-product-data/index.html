How to Scrape Daraz Product Data: Prices, Sellers and Stock (2026 Guide)

Step-by-step guide to scraping Daraz products in Bangladesh, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Nepal and Myanmar. Export prices, discounts, sellers and stock to CSV or Excel without code.

By the Scraplex team at ITclan BDUpdated 23 Nov 20267 min read
Short answerOpen the Daraz Product Scraper on Apify, choose a country domain (bd, pk, lk, np or mm), add search keywords or category URLs, and press Start. Each product comes back with its price, discount, seller, rating, sold count and stock status, ready to export as CSV, Excel or JSON. It costs $1.00 per 1,000 products.

Daraz is the largest online marketplace in South Asia, with storefronts in Bangladesh, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Nepal and Myanmar. If you sell on Daraz, supply brands that do, or research the market, product data from Daraz tells you who sells what, at what price, and how fast it moves.

Daraz has no public product API, so copying data by hand is the usual fallback. This guide shows how to collect it automatically with a ready-made scraper, without writing code.

What data you can get from Daraz

The Daraz Product Scraper reads Daraz search and category pages and returns one row per product:

GroupFields
Producttitle, description, productId, sku, brand, categoryIds
Pricingprice, originalPrice, discountPct, currency
SellersellerName, sellerId, location
Social proofrating, reviewCount, soldCount
StatusinStock, isSponsored, badges
LinksimageUrl, productUrl, country, scrapedAt

Prices come back in the local currency of each market: BDT, PKR, LKR, NPR or MMK.

How to scrape Daraz in 5 steps

  1. Open the scraper. Go to the Daraz Product Scraper on Apify and sign in with a free Apify account.
  2. Pick the market. Set country_domain to bd, pk, lk, np or mm.
  3. Add what to search. Enter keywords such as "earbuds" in search_keywords, or paste up to 100 category or search URLs in startUrls.
  4. Set filters and limits. Sort by popularity or price, set a minimum rating or a price range, and choose how many products or pages to collect.
  5. Run and export. Press Start. When the run finishes, download the results as CSV, Excel or JSON, or send them to Google Sheets.

Example input

This input collects up to 500 earbuds from Daraz Bangladesh, sorted by popularity, with a minimum 4-star rating:

{
  "country_domain": "bd",
  "search_keywords": ["earbuds"],
  "sortBy": "popularity",
  "minRating": 4,
  "maxProducts": 500
}

Tips for complete, clean results

  • Use category URLs for full coverage. Keyword searches return what Daraz ranks for that word. A category URL returns the whole category.
  • Keep out-of-stock items for research. includeOutOfStock is on by default, which shows you demand that sellers are not meeting.
  • Choose pages or products as the limit. maxPages is useful for quick samples. maxProducts is better when you need an exact row count. Set it to 0 for no limit.
  • Schedule it. A daily run turns one export into a price history you can chart.

How much it costs

The scraper charges $1.00 per 1,000 products. Collecting 10,000 products costs about $10, and Apify's free plan covers small test runs. Higher Apify plans get a discount on the per-result price.

Is scraping Daraz allowed?

The scraper collects product information that Daraz shows publicly to every visitor. You are responsible for how you use the data, so check Daraz's terms and local rules for your use case, and avoid collecting personal data you have no reason to hold.

FAQ

Can I scrape Daraz reviews too?

Yes, with the separate Daraz Product Review Scraper. The product scraper returns ratings and review counts, not review text.

Does it work with product detail pages?

No. Use search, category or catalog URLs. To collect data for specific products, search by the product name or use the review scraper with the product URL.

Can I get the data through an API?

Yes. Every run is available through the Apify API, and you can call the scraper from Python or JavaScript.
