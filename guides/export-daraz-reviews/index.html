How to Export Daraz Product Reviews to CSV or Excel

Export Daraz product reviews with ratings, review text, photos and votes to CSV or Excel. Filter by star rating and sort by most recent. Works in all 5 Daraz countries.

By the Scraplex team at ITclan BDUpdated 21 Nov 20266 min read
Short answerPaste one or more Daraz product URLs into the Daraz Product Review Scraper on Apify, set the matching country (BD, PK, LK, MM or NP), choose a sort order and star filter, and run it. You get every review with its rating, text, date, photos and votes, exported to CSV, Excel or JSON for $1.00 per 1,000 reviews.

Reviews are the most honest data on Daraz. They tell you what buyers like, what breaks, and what sellers promise but do not deliver. Reading them one page at a time on the site is slow, and Daraz offers no review export.

The Daraz Product Review Scraper collects them for you and puts every review in one spreadsheet.

Why export Daraz reviews

  • Product research: check what buyers complain about before you stock a product.
  • Quality control: pull every 1 and 2 star review for your own listings each week.
  • Competitor analysis: find the repeated complaints in rival products and fix them in yours.
  • Sentiment scoring: feed review text into an AI model or BI tool.

How to export reviews step by step

  1. Copy the URLs of the Daraz products you want to analyse.
  2. Open the Daraz Product Review Scraper on Apify and paste them into urls.
  3. Set country to the market that matches the URLs: BD for daraz.com.bd, PK for daraz.pk, LK for daraz.lk, NP for daraz.com.np, MM for shop.com.mm.
  4. Choose a sort order and, if you want, a star filter.
  5. Press Start, then export the dataset as CSV or Excel.

Sorting and star filters

SettingValuesUse it for
sorting_order0 relevance, 1 most recent, 2 rating high to low, 3 rating low to highUse 1 for weekly monitoring, so new reviews come first
star_filter0 all, or 1 to 5Use 1 or 2 to collect only complaints

This input collects only 1-star reviews from a product on Daraz Pakistan, newest first:

{
  "urls": ["https://www.daraz.pk/products/..."],
  "country": "PK",
  "sorting_order": 1,
  "star_filter": 1
}

What to do with the data

Start simple. In Excel or Google Sheets, count reviews by rating and by month to see if quality is improving. Then search the review text for words like "broken", "fake" or "late" to group complaints. For larger volumes, run the text through a sentiment model or ask an AI assistant to summarise the top five complaints.

To compare products, pair this export with the Daraz Product Scraper, which gives you price, sold count and seller for the same items.

FAQ

How much does it cost?

$1.00 per 1,000 reviews. A product with 2,000 reviews costs about $2 to export in full.

Are reviewer names shown in full?

Daraz masks most names on its own pages, and the scraper returns them the way Daraz shows them.

Can I export reviews for many products at once?

Yes. Paste as many product URLs as you need into one run. They must all be from the same country domain.
