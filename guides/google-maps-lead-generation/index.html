How to Scrape Google Maps for Local Business Leads (With Reviews)

Build local B2B lead lists from Google Maps: business names, phones, websites, ratings and reviews by city and category. Step-by-step guide with example inputs.

By the Scraplex team at ITclan BDUpdated 17 Nov 20268 min read
Short answerOpen the Google Maps Places & Reviews Scraper on Apify, enter a business type such as "dentist" and a location such as "Austin, TX", set how many places and reviews you want, and run it. Each place comes back with its name, address, phone, website, rating, hours and reviews, ready to export to CSV or your CRM. It starts at $3.00 per 1,000 results.

Almost every local business has a Google Maps listing, and most keep their phone number, website and opening hours up to date because customers find them there. That makes Google Maps one of the most current lead sources for agencies, SaaS companies and B2B sales teams that sell to local businesses.

Why Google Maps is a strong lead source

  • Coverage: restaurants, clinics, salons, contractors, shops and offices in nearly every city.
  • Freshness: owners update their own listings, so contact details are often more current than bought lists.
  • Built-in qualification: ratings, review counts and claim status tell you how established and active a business is.

How to build a lead list step by step

  1. Define your ideal customer. Pick the business type and the cities you sell to, for example dentists in Austin.
  2. Set up the search. Enter "dentist" in searchTerms and "Austin, TX" in locationQuery.
  3. Set the volume. Use maxPlacesPerSearch to control results per search, and maxPlacesTotal to cap the whole run.
  4. Decide on reviews. Set maxReviewsPerPlace to 0 for a contact list only, or 10 to 20 to read what customers say.
  5. Run, export and clean. Export to CSV, remove places without a website or phone, and import the rest into your CRM.
{
  "searchTerms": ["dentist", "orthodontist"],
  "locationQuery": "Austin, TX",
  "maxPlacesPerSearch": 100,
  "maxReviewsPerPlace": 10,
  "language": "en"
}

How to qualify leads with ratings and reviews

Not every listing is a good prospect. Some simple filters help:

  • Unclaimed listings are good leads for local SEO and reputation services.
  • Low ratings with many reviews point to businesses that need help with service or reputation.
  • No website is the clearest signal for web design agencies.
  • Owner replies show an engaged owner who is more likely to respond to outreach.

Using the data responsibly

Business listings are public, but outreach rules still apply. Follow anti-spam and privacy laws in the countries you contact, such as CAN-SPAM in the US and GDPR in the EU, and give people an easy way to opt out. The scraper is an independent tool and is not affiliated with Google.

FAQ

How much does a lead list cost?

From $3.00 per 1,000 results. A list of 1,000 businesses costs about $3, plus any reviews you choose to include. Check the pricing tab on Apify for current rates.

Does it return email addresses?

No. It returns the phone and website shown on the listing. Many teams then find emails from the website, which we can add in a custom feed.

Can I search in other languages?

Yes. It supports 74 languages through the language setting.
